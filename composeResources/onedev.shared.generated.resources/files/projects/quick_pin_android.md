# Quick Pin Android

**Quick Pin** lets you pin any file — PDFs, images, documents, and Google Drive files — directly to your Android home screen as a beautiful, fully customizable widget.

## Stop digging through folders just to open a file you use every day.

- 📌 PIN ANYTHING
    - Local files: PDF, PNG, JPG, DOCX, and more
    - Google Drive files and folders
    - Any file you open daily, right at your fingertips

- 🧩 POWERFUL HOME SCREEN WIDGETS
  - Add a widget once, access your files instantly — no app-switching, no searching. Quick Pin widgets update automatically so your pinned content is always fresh.

- 🎨 FULLY CUSTOMIZABLE WIDGETS
 - Make your home screen yours. Choose from multiple view types:
   - List view — clean and scannable
   - Grid view — visual and compact
   - Tab view — organize by category
   - Single view — spotlight one file

Customize colors, sizes, labels, and more to match your style and workflow.

⚡ BUILT FOR SPEED
Quick Pin is designed for people who value their time. One tap on your widget opens the exact file you need — instantly.

🔒 SIMPLE & PRIVATE
No sign-up required for local files. Your files stay on your device.
Perfect for students, professionals, designers, and anyone who works with files daily.
